08 — AccessibilityLayer 08 / 08

Polish includeseveryone.

These rules are built into the system, so later pages can’t forget them.

  • Readable contrastAll text is at least 4.5 : 1, and the smallest gray is 5.3 : 1. White never sits on orange.
  • Color is never the only signalCurrent page = underline. Current layer = longer tick + number. Selected filter = check icon. Inline links are always underlined.
  • Motion is optionalFollows your system setting, with a manual toggle. Pinned scenes become plain stacked content, and nothing auto-plays or loops.
  • Keyboard firstSkip link, a visible orange focus ring, a slider in place of the hover lens, and a native dialog menu (Esc closes it and focus returns).
  • Real structureLandmarks, one h1, labelled sections. Screenshots carry text alternatives, and decorative layers are hidden from screen readers.
  • Comfortable targetsInteractive elements are 40–48px tall, and at least 44px on touch screens.
